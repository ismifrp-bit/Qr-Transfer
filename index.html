QR Transfer

Transfer data without internet.

Scan. Transfer. Done. Everything happens directly between two devices, through the camera — nothing touches a server.

100% client-side No internet needed Nothing stored in a cloud

How it works

  1. Pick data on the sending device — text, a link, or a file.
  2. QR codes appear on screen, one after another if the data is large.
  3. Point the other device's camera at the screen to scan.
  4. It's reassembled and checked automatically, then ready to open.

Works well for

Phone → phone Laptop → phone Notes & messages Links Photos Small documents Contact cards
